Privacy Policy for AlphaBlast

Last Updated: September 2026

At AlphaBlast (developed and published by Alexander Castillo), accessible via our web application and mobile application, the privacy of our visitors and players is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AlphaBlast and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at alx.mobileapps@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you play AlphaBlast, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Scores, categories unlocked, stars, coins, and local game state (stored locally on your device or synced anonymously via Google Cloud Firestore).
  • Device and Usage Information: Browser type, operating system version, device model, language preferences, IP address, and interaction timestamps.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Anonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android devices or browser cookies) used solely to serve relevant ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining the AlphaBlast word puzzle game.
  • Displaying high scores, leaderboards, and category progression.
  • Monitoring, analyzing, and improving gameplay performance and fixing bugs.
  • Displaying non-intrusive advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads) to keep the game free to play.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdSense & AdMob)

AlphaBlast uses third-party advertising services provided by Google LLC, including Google AdSense and Google AdMob.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and advertising identifiers to serve ads on AlphaBlast based on prior visits to our site or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our game and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) is used to gather consent for advertising and analytics cookies in compliance with GDPR.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other web app, AlphaBlast uses 'cookies' and local storage (localStorage) to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages or game levels accessed. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and device settings.

AlphaBlast's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information:

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data collected.
  • Request that a business not sell or share the consumer's personal data.

We do not sell personal data. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact us at alx.mobileapps@gmail.com.

7. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing.

8. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. AlphaBlast does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our game, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information.
